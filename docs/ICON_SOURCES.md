# Replacement icon sources

Applied on 2026-10-24 after owner approval of the preview. This list covers 27 replacement resources: 15 adaptations of Tabler Icons and 12 original geometric drawings. The original project code license remains undecided. The artwork MIT notice applies only to the 12 original drawings listed here.

Tabler upstream revision: `a4ce1404bc6d24d3c365afe7b258d6bf6f48d62d`. Retain its copyright and MIT notice when distributing the adapted assets. Adaptations include recoloring, fills, composition, stroke adjustments, canvas fitting and PNG rasterization. Editable weather SVG sources are retained under `docs/artwork-sources/weather/` under the same Tabler MIT notice. Weather PNGs retain their previous pixel sizes and resource paths; SVGs retain their previous viewBoxes and explicit dimensions. The plane artwork is rotated -90 degrees around its center so heading zero still points north. QML layout, animation bindings and rotation origins were not changed.
